  • flour 100 g
  • sugar 200 g

  • milk 500 ml ~515g
  • butter 50 g
  • vanilla bean 1 — split and scraped
  • egg yolk (egg) 4 ~72g

  1. Mix In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, the sugar, and the salt. dry ingredients
  2. Heat In a saucepan, bring the milk, the butter, and the vanilla bean to 85 °C. hot milk
  3. Mix Pour the hot milk over the dry ingredients in one go. Whisk vigorously.
  4. Add Add the egg yolk and the rum.
  5. Rest Cover with cling film in contact and refrigerate for at least 24 h.

Molding

  • batter
  1. Line Butter the cannelé molds generously.
  2. Fill Fill the molds to 3/4 with the batter. Bake at 180 °C for 50 min.
