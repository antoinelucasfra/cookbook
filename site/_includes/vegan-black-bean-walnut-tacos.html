Filling >608g

  • olive-oil 15 ml ~13.7g
  • onion 110 g — finely chopped
  • garlic 6 g — minced
  • cumin 4 g
  • chili-powder 2 g — ancho
  • paprika 2 g
  • cayenne-pepper
  • walnuts 70 g — finely chopped
  • black-beans 260 g — drained
  • canned-tomatoes 120 g — tomato sauce
  • lime-juice 15 ml ~15.5g
  • cilantro 5 g — chopped
  • salt
  • black-pepper
  1. Sweat Coat the bottom of a large skillet with the olive-oil and place over medium heat. Add the onion and sweat, stirring occasionally, until soft and translucent, 5 min.
  2. Bloom Stir in the garlic, the cumin, the chili-powder, the paprika, a pinch of cayenne-pepper and the walnuts. Sauté, stirring constantly, until very fragrant, 1 min.
  3. Simmer Stir in the black-beans and the canned-tomatoes. Raise the heat and bring to a simmer.
  4. Braise Lower the heat and simmer until thick, stirring occasionally. Optionally mash a few beans against the side of the skillet to help thicken the sauce, 10 min.
  5. Finish Remove from heat. Stir in the lime-juice and the cilantro. Season with salt and black-pepper to taste.

Serve

  • corn-tortillas
  1. Stuff Stuff the filling into warmed corn-tortillas and top with shredded cabbage, salsa or guacamole.
