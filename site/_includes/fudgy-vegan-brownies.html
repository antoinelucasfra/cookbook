  1. Soak Whisk the flaxseed with the water in a bowl and leave to thicken while you prepare the rest, 10 min.
  2. Preheat Preheat the oven to 177 °C. Line a 9×9 square baking dish with parchment paper.
  3. Combine Sift the flour and the cocoa into a mixing bowl. Add the baking-powder and the salt and mix.
  4. Melt Add the vegan-butter and the chocolate-chips to a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring between each, until melted.
  5. Mix Whisk the sugar, the vanilla-extract and the soaked flaxseed into the melted chocolate.
  6. Fold Pour the wet mixture over the dry ingredients and mix into a thick batter. Fold in the remaining chocolate-chips.

Bake

  • chocolate-chips
  1. Bake Transfer to the prepared baking dish and smooth down. Press a few extra chocolate-chips on top. Bake until dry and shiny on top and a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs, 40 min.
  2. Cool Cool in the pan, 15 min, then lift out with the parchment overhang and cool completely before slicing.
