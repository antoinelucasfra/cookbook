  • lemon-juice
  1. Blend Place the chickpeas, the tahini, the water, the garlic, the lemon-juice, the cumin and a pinch of salt in a food processor. Blend until smooth and creamy, 2 min. Scrape down the sides as needed.
  2. Adjust Taste and adjust: more lemon-juice, salt or water for a thinner consistency. Blend again briefly.

Serve >14g

  • olive-oil 15 ml ~13.7g
  • chickpeas
  • sesame-seeds
  • paprika
  1. Serve Spread in a shallow bowl and drizzle with olive-oil. Top with a few chickpeas, a sprinkle of sesame-seeds or paprika.
