Pesto >326g

  • pine-nuts 80 g
  • basil 90 g
  • lemon-juice 15 ml ~15.5g
  • olive-oil 80 ml ~72.8g
  • pine-nuts
  • garlic 3 g — 1 clove
  • nutritional-yeast 5 g
  • water 60 ml ~60g
  1. Toast Lightly toast the pine-nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat, 3 min.
  2. Blend Place the basil, the lemon-juice, the olive-oil, the toasted pine-nuts, the garlic, the nutritional-yeast and the water in a food processor. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides. Set aside.

Pasta ~942g

  • salt 8 g
  • pasta 400 g — calamarata
  • water 120 ml ~120g
  • olive-oil 20 ml ~18.2g
  • garlic 6 g — 2 cloves
  • asparagus 150 g — tips
  • butter-beans 240 g
  1. Boil Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil over high heat. Add the salt and the pasta. Cook according to package timing, stirring occasionally. Reserve the pasta cooking water, then drain.
  2. Sauté Heat the olive-oil in a large pan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and the asparagus and sauté until softened without darkening, 60 s.
  3. Toss Add the drained butter-beans, the cooked pasta and the pesto to the pan. Toss continuously until evenly coated, loosening with reserved cooking water a few tablespoons at a time.

  • basil
  • parsley
  1. Serve Divide into bowls and finish with extra basil, chopped parsley and vegan parmesan.
