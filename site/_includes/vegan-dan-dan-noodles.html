Tofu ~289g

  • tofu 225 g
  • soy-sauce 30 ml ~34.5g
  • hoisin-sauce 15 g
  • five-spice 1 g
  • peanut-oil 15 ml ~13.8g
  1. Dice Cut the tofu into 5 mm cubes.
  2. Mix Stir the soy-sauce, the hoisin-sauce and the five-spice together. tofu seasoning
  3. Fry Heat the peanut-oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the tofu until browned, 8 min.
  4. Glaze Drizzle over the tofu seasoning and cook until the liquid evaporates, 1 min.

Noodles ~685g

  • fresh-noodles 450 g
  • spinach 170 g
  • dan dan sauce
  • roasted-peanuts 35 g
  • scallions 2 ~30g
  1. Boil Cook the fresh-noodles in boiling water per package directions. Add the spinach for the last 30 s, then drain everything together.
  2. Assemble Divide the dan dan sauce between four bowls. Top with the noodles, the glazed tofu, the roasted-peanuts and the scallions.
