Rice >575g

  • basmati-rice 200 g 200g
  • olive-oil 15 ml ~13.7g
  • cloves
  • cardamom 1 g 1g
  • water 360 ml ~360g
  1. Soak Soak the basmati-rice, 30 min. Rinse under cold water and drain.
  2. Toast Heat the olive-oil in a saucepan over high heat. Fry the cloves and the cardamom, 30 s.
  3. Steam Add the drained rice and toast, stirring, 60 s. Pour in the water, cover, and when boiling reduce to low. Steam until the rice is cooked and all water is absorbed, 18 min.

Vegetables >875g

  • potato 180 g 180g
  • carrot 60 g 60g
  • vegan-butter 43 g 43g
  • cauliflower 150 g 150g — florets
  • green-beans 120 g 120g — chopped
  • green-chili 15 g 15g — minced
  • ginger 8 g 8g — grated
  • cumin 1.5 g 1.5g
  • turmeric 3 g 3g
  • chili-powder 2 g 2g — Kashmiri
  • curry-leaves
  • tomato 150 g 150g
  • peas 90 g 90g
  • cashews 50 g 50g — toasted
  • rosewater 2.5 ml ~2.5g
  1. Boil Meanwhile, boil the diced potato and carrot in a pot of salted water until fork-tender, 13 min. Drain.
  2. Sauté Melt the vegan-butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté the cauliflower, the green-beans, the green-chili and the ginger, 5 min.
  3. Spice Add the cumin, the turmeric, the chili-powder and the curry-leaves. Stir to coat, 1 min.
  4. Simmer Stir in the chopped tomato, the peas and the boiled potato and carrot. Cover and cook over low heat until the vegetables are tender, 10 min.
  5. Layer Fluff the rice with a fork and fold it through the vegetables along with the cashews.
  6. Finish Sprinkle with rosewater.

Serve

  • cilantro
  • mint
  1. Serve Garnish with fresh cilantro and mint. Serve hot.
