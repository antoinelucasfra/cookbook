Roast >617g

  • butternut-squash 600 g — peeled, cubed
  • olive-oil 15 ml ~13.7g
  • garlic 15 ml — crushed
  • paprika 1 g — smoked
  • salt 1 g
  • black-pepper 1/2 g
  • cayenne-pepper 1/2 g
  1. Preheat Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Toss Add the butternut-squash to a mixing bowl with the olive-oil, the garlic, the paprika, the salt, the black-pepper and the cayenne-pepper and toss well.
  3. Roast Spread evenly on a parchment-lined baking tray and roast until tender and roasted, 35 min.

Sauce ~627g

  • cashews 100 g — raw
  • nutritional-yeast 12 g
  • garlic-powder 2 g
  • onion-powder 2 g
  • dijon-mustard 15 ml ~15.8g
  • lemon-juice 15 ml ~15.5g
  • vegetable-stock 240 ml ~240g — 1 cup
  • vegetable-stock 240 ml ~240g
  1. Blend While the squash roasts, add the cashews, the nutritional-yeast, the garlic-powder, the onion-powder, the dijon-mustard, the lemon-juice and vegetable-stock to a blender. Blend until smooth.
  2. Finish sauce When the squash is roasted, add it to the blender with the remaining vegetable-stock and blend until you have a smooth sauce.

Serve >450g

  • pasta 450 g — macaroni
  • parsley
  • black-pepper
  1. Cook Cook the pasta in salted boiling water in a large pot and drain. 10 min
  2. Toss Return the pasta to the pot, pour over the sauce and gently toss until well coated. Stir over low-medium heat to warm everything through, 3 min.
  3. Serve Top with chopped parsley and ground black-pepper.
