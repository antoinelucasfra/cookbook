Cashew base ~290g

  • cashews 110 g 110g
  • vegetable-stock 180 ml ~180g
  1. Blend Place the cashews and the vegetable-stock in a food processor. Blend to a smooth paste.

Curry >1251g

  • vegan-butter 28 g 28g
  • onion 150 g 150g — finely chopped
  • seitan 448 g 448g — torn into bite-sized pieces
  • garlic 9 g 9g — minced
  • ginger 8 g 8g — grated
  • garam-masala 7 g 7g
  • cumin 2 g 2g
  • paprika 2 g 2g
  • vegetable-stock 180 ml ~180g
  • canned-tomatoes 400 g 400g
  • tomato-paste 17 g 17g
  • salt
  • cilantro
  1. Sweat Melt the vegan-butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and sweat until soft and translucent, 5 min.
  2. Brown Add the seitan. Cook, stirring frequently, until it begins to brown, 5 min.
  3. Bloom Stir in the garlic, the ginger, the garam-masala, the cumin and the paprika. Cook until very fragrant, 1 min.
  4. Simmer Stir in the remaining vegetable-stock, the canned-tomatoes and the tomato-paste. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, 10 min.
  5. Finish Stir in the cashew paste and cook until the sauce comes back up to a simmer. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Sprinkle with fresh cilantro.

Serve 300g

  • basmati-rice 300 g 300g — cooked
  1. Serve Serve over basmati-rice.
