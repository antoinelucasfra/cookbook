Vegetables ~430g

  • olive-oil 30 ml ~27.3g
  • onion 150 g 150g — chopped
  • celery 100 g 100g — sliced
  • carrot 120 g 120g — sliced
  • garlic 12 g 12g — crushed
  • chili-powder 15 g 15g
  • cumin 2 g 2g
  • oregano 1 g 1g
  • cayenne-pepper 1/2 g 0.5g
  • smoked-paprika 2 g 2g
  1. Sweat Heat the olive-oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté the onion, the celery and the carrot until softened, 10 min.
  2. Bloom Add the garlic, the chili-powder, the cumin, the oregano, the cayenne-pepper and the smoked-paprika. Cook until fragrant, 2 min.

Simmer >1629g

  • canned-tomatoes 784 g 784g
  • vegetable-stock 240 ml ~240g
  • tomato-paste 60 g 60g
  • kidney-beans 260 g 260g
  • black-beans 260 g 260g
  • brown-sugar 25 g 25g
  • salt
  1. Simmer Add the canned-tomatoes, the vegetable-stock, the tomato-paste, the kidney-beans and the black-beans. Stir and bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Braise Reduce the heat and simmer uncovered until the vegetables are cooked and the flavors have blended, 30 min. Stir occasionally.
  3. Season Stir in the brown-sugar. Season with salt to taste.

Serve

  • avocado
  • cilantro
  1. Serve Serve topped with diced avocado and fresh cilantro.
