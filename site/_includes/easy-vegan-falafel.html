Dough >431g

  • chickpeas 250 g — drained
  • parsley 15 g — chopped
  • shallot 100 g — roughly chopped
  • garlic 12 g — minced
  • sesame-seeds 18 g
  • cumin 3 g
  • coriander 1 g
  • cardamom 1/2 g
  • salt 1 g
  • black pepper
  • flour 30 g
  1. Blend Add the chickpeas, the parsley, the shallot, the garlic, the sesame-seeds, the cumin, the coriander, the cardamom, the salt and the black pepper to a food processor. Pulse to a crumbly dough — not a paste.
  2. Bind Add the flour one tablespoon (~8 g) at a time, pulsing after each, until the dough holds together when molded into a ball without sticking.
  3. Chill Transfer to a mixing bowl, cover and refrigerate, 60 min. This firms up the dough.

Shape & fry ~41g

  • vegetable-oil 45 ml ~41.4g
  1. Shape Scoop rounded tablespoons (~30 g) and form into 11–12 small discs.
  2. Fry Heat the vegetable-oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Fry the falafel in batches until deep golden brown on both sides, 5 min total per batch. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Crisp Optional: finish in a preheated 204 °Coven, 8 min, for extra crispiness.

Serve

  1. Serve Serve warm in pita bread with hummus and a garlic-herb sauce.
