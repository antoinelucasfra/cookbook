Bolognese >935g

  • mushrooms 180 g 180g
  • tofu 224 g 224g — extra-firm
  • walnuts 70 g 70g — processed into crumbs
  • onion 55 g 55g — chopped
  • garlic 15 ml — crushed
  • olive-oil 15 ml ~13.7g
  • oregano-dried 2 g 2g
  • basil 1 g 1g — dried
  • cayenne-pepper 1/2 g 0.5g
  • soy-sauce 15 ml ~17.3g — dark
  • tomato-paste 120 g 120g
  • marinara-sauce 240 g 240g
  • sugar 12 g 12g — coconut
  • salt
  • black-pepper
  1. Process Slice the mushrooms and process in a food processor until very finely chopped. Add to a mixing bowl. Crumble in the tofu and add the walnuts. Mix together.
  2. Sauté Add the onion to a pot with the garlic, the olive-oil, the oregano-dried, the basil and the cayenne-pepper and sauté until softened, 3 min.
  3. Braise Add the mushroom mix, the soy-sauce, the tomato-paste and the marinara-sauce. Sauté until you have a soft bolognese with no visible liquid. Balance with the sugar and season with salt and black-pepper.

Cheese sauce ~1179g

  • olive-oil 90 ml ~81.9g
  • flour 40 g 40g
  • soy-milk 960 ml ~988.8g
  • dijon-mustard 30 ml ~31.5g
  • nutritional-yeast 25 g 25g
  • onion-powder 3 g 3g
  • garlic-powder 3 g 3g
  • paprika 1 g 1g — smoked
  • salt 3 g 3g
  • black-pepper 2 g 2g
  1. Roux Heat the olive-oil in a pot. Add the flour and fry briefly, stirring vigorously. Pour in all of the soy-milk, whisking to remove lumps.
  2. Thicken Bring to the boil stirring constantly and let it boil until thickened, 3 min. Off heat, stir in the dijon-mustard, the nutritional-yeast, the onion-powder, the garlic-powder, the paprika, the salt and the black-pepper.

Assemble >400g

  • marinara-sauce
  • lasagna-sheets
  • zucchini — 4 small
  • vegan-ricotta 400 g 400g
  • zucchini
  1. Preheat Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Layer In a deep 9×13 baking dish, layer: thin layer of marinara-sauce, 4 lasagna-sheets, half the bolognese spread evenly, thin layer of cheese sauce, sliced zucchini, all of the vegan-ricotta, then repeat: 4 lasagna-sheets, more marinara-sauce, thin layer of cheese sauce, remaining bolognese, sliced zucchini, final 4 lasagna-sheets and remaining marinara-sauce.
  3. Bake covered Cover with foil and bake, 30 min.
  4. Bake open Remove foil, pour the rest of the cheese sauce on top and return to the oven uncovered, 20 min.

Serve

  • basil
  • black-pepper
  1. Rest Test with a knife that the sheets are cooked through. Drizzle the middle with marinara and decorate with chopped basil and ground black-pepper.
